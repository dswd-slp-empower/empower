EMPOWER

DSWD Point of Sale System

A mobile app for sari-sari store operators in DSWD livelihood associations to manage inventory, sales, and finances on a single shared device — no internet required.

Offline-First DSWD SLP Android

Key Features

PIN Authentication

Secure 4-digit PIN login for members without internet or passwords.

Inventory Management

Stock-in, stock-out, FIFO lot tracking, unit conversions, and transformations.

Sales Recording

Cash and credit sales with product selection, customer tracking, and history.

Expenses & Payables

Track store expenses and owner payables with categorized reporting.

Customer Credit

Manage utang with credit limits, payment tracking, and auto-adjustment rates.

Financial Reports

Generate income statements, balance sheets, cash flow, and PDF exports.

Google Drive Backup

Optional automatic backup to Google Drive with configurable intervals.

Audit Trail

All create, update, and delete actions logged with member attribution.

About This Application

E.M.P.O.W.E.R is a Flutter-based mobile application for Android developed as a capstone project by students of Cebu Technological University — Ginatilan Extension Campus for the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) Sustainable Livelihood Program (SLP).

The app is designed for SLP Associations (SLPAs) — small retail store groups in the Philippines — to manage day-to-day operations from a single shared device. All data is stored locally on the device using SQLite, with optional Google Drive backup for disaster recovery.

How It Works

E.M.P.O.W.E.R is a single-device, offline-first application. One device per organization holds the full database locally. Multiple association members can log in on that device using their 4-digit PINs.

  • Local only: All features work without internet. Data is stored in a local SQLite database.
  • Online features: Google Drive backup and restore (optional) and product modification email alerts require internet.
  • Backup: Full database snapshots can be uploaded to Google Drive manually or on a configurable automatic schedule (default 60 minutes). Backups are ZIP archives containing the database, images, and metadata.
  • Restore: Backup archives can be downloaded and restored to the same or a different device, replacing all local data.
Google Drive: Backup uses the drive.file OAuth scope — the app can only access files it creates in your Google Drive.
