1. Description of the App

E.M.P.O.W.E.R is a mobile application designed for sari-sari store management. The App operates primarily offline and stores data locally on your device. It is intended for use by DSWD Sustainable Livelihood Program (SLP) Associations (SLPAs) and their members.

The App is not a web platform or cloud service. Core features function without internet access. Online features are limited to optional Google Drive backup/restore and product modification email alerts.

The App offers an optional Google Drive backup feature. Key terms:
